<section class="research-section" id="research-program">
  <p class="eyebrow">RESEARCH PROGRAM</p>
  <h2>Discovery → Interpretation → Translation</h2>
  <p>My research moves beyond the one-gene–one-protein view to resolve how transcript variation reshapes protein structure, function, and therapeutic opportunity. I conceived and lead an integrated program spanning atlas construction, functional inference, and actionable target prioritization.</p>
  <div class="research-grid research-grid--three">
